Questions Before Choosing A Professional

When comparing conveyancing services, look beyond the quoted professional fee. Published provider entries advise contacting each business directly to confirm their scope, fees, availability, and whether their practitioners can assist with your specific matter. Use this contact to gauge the specificity of their response to your file. Inquire about who will handle day-to-day communication, which documents will be reviewed, how questions will be explained, and what third-party charges might be separate from the professional fee. If a contract has been signed, state this upfront. If a fixed date exists, provide it. Clearly identify commercial, off-the-plan, or transfer matters rather than broadly describing them as property transactions.
